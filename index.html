About
Jhon

"No tengo prisa. Tengo dirección."
I'm not in a hurry. I have direction.

I'm a Systems Engineering student (UNAD, 8th semester) and QA Manual Tester at Nelumbo Consultores in Cúcuta, Colombia. I grew up in Arboledas, Norte de Santander, and built my way into tech through construction work, online education, and stubborn persistence.

My path wasn't linear — it was construction sites, failed semesters, a near-dismissal at my first real tech job, and a motorcycle I saved up for while pouring cement. Every detour was a lesson I now draw from daily.
